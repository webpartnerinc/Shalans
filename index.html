Welcome to the neighborhood

A Main Street favorite in Utah’s turkey country

Shalan’s sits right on Main Street in Moroni, a small Sanpete County town ringed by turkey farms and wide valley views. Travelers rolling along U.S. Route 89 stumble onto it, locals swear by it, and first-timers usually become regulars by the second visit. It is the kind of cozy, counter-service spot where the coffee stays hot and everyone is greeted like a neighbor.

The kitchen keeps things homestyle and honest: griddle-fresh breakfasts from 7 AM, hearty sandwiches stacked on panini or croissant bread, crisp salads for the lighter appetite, and a sweet lineup of soft-serve, flavored hot chocolate and smoothies for the road. Scan the reviews and a theme appears fast — the food is fresh, the soft-serve is a steal, and the folks behind the window are genuinely glad you came.

In a hurry? The drive-thru order window keeps passing traffic moving, while a few comfortable tables and TVs inside make it an easy stop for families with kids. Whether it is an early breakfast burrito before work or a milkshake for the drive home, Shalan’s does small-town comfort right.
