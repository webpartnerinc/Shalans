Small town, easy detour

Getting here is half the fun

Moroni sits in the heart of Sanpete County, where U.S. Route 89 rolls through some of central Utah’s prettiest farm country. The town is famous for its turkey farms — so it’s only fitting that turkey shows up front and center on the plates at Shalan’s, too.

Driving between Nephi and Manti? You’ll pass right through Moroni. Watch for Main Street, and pull in when you spot the retro oval sign with the two red-haired kids and their milkshake — the drive-thru window is ready for road-trippers, and there’s room to park while you eat inside.

Make it part of the day trip: Sanpete Valley is dotted with historic towns, mountain views and heritage sites, and Shalan’s makes a dependable stop for breakfast on the way out or a shake on the way home.

Good to know

  • Drive-thru window — order without leaving the car.
  • Takeout available — everything packs up neatly for the road.
  • Cozy seating & TVs inside if you’d rather stay a while.
  • Family-friendly — kids love the milkshake lineup.
  • Big group? Call (435) 427-3689 ahead and they’ll get it ready.
  • Holiday hours vary — check before a Sunday or holiday visit.
